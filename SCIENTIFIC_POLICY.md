# AXIS scientific use policy

AXIS generates research hypotheses. It does not establish causality, validate a
drug, recommend treatment or replace statistical, biological, clinical or
ethical review.

## Required evidence discipline

- The participant is the statistical unit unless another independent unit is
  explicitly justified.
- Technical replicates, cells and repeated samples must not inflate sample size.
- Discovery, independent validation, sensitivity, mechanism and treatment
  response must remain separately labelled.
- Tissue, cell type, assay, disease definition, treatment and study design must
  be reviewed before effects are combined.
- Incompatible effect scales must not be pooled merely to increase study count.
- Expression association must not be presented as causal genetic evidence.
- Structural or pharmacological evidence must not convert an unvalidated gene
  into a therapeutic recommendation.
- Negative, contradictory and missing evidence must remain visible.
- Every publishable result must identify its inputs, transformations, method,
  software version and checksums where practical.

## Human and AI review

Automated or AI-generated eligibility decisions and interpretations are
proposals until reviewed by a responsible researcher. AI output is not a source
of scientific evidence. Authors remain accountable for claims made with AXIS.

## Data responsibility

Only data compatible with their consent, repository terms and applicable law
may be processed. Public availability does not remove the duty to minimize data,
protect privacy and cite the originating investigators.
