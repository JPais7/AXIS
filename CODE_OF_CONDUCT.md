# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive and harassment-free project for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and constructive.
- Critique evidence and code, not people.
- Declare uncertainty, conflicts of interest and relevant limitations.
- Respect participant privacy, data-use conditions and research ethics.
- Accept correction when claims exceed the available evidence.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate
misinformation and publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `joaopais2000@hotmail.com` with the subject
`AXIS conduct report`. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented and protect the
privacy of reporters.

This project code of conduct is informed by the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
