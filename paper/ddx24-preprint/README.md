# DDX24 preprint package

The primary question is whether donor-level DDX24 expression is lower in
peripheral CD8 T-cell populations from people with axial or ankylosing
spondylitis than in healthy controls.

The primary synthesis contains two compatible independent CD8 cohorts, 14
cases and 33 controls. The random-effects estimate is -0.148 log2-CPM (95%
confidence interval -0.272 to -0.024; p=0.019). A third independent broad-CD8
cohort is sensitivity-only evidence; the 51-participant estimate is -0.145
(95% confidence interval -0.249 to -0.041; p=0.006).

Five additional blood cohorts provide contextual, not interchangeable,
evidence. Their microarray and RNA-sequencing effects are not pooled with the
CD8 estimate because assay scales and cellular compositions differ.

The permitted interpretation is an association that merits prospective
falsification. The study does not establish causality, diagnostic validity,
druggability, therapeutic direction, efficacy or safety.

AXIS version 0.2.0 is archived at
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21762169. The Word manuscript is a preprint draft
requiring final approval by both authors and independent scientific review
before journal submission.
